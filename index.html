Questions, answered

Do I need to know how to code?

No. If you can describe what you need, AI can build it with you. Anyone is welcome.

Who should come?

Solo founders, teams, and early-stage and late-stage companies. If something in your business eats a few hours every week, bring it.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. The hackathon is free to attend. Space is limited and we review every RSVP, so register early on Luma.

Can I come alone, or should I bring a team?

Either works. Build solo or bring your team.

What should I bring?

A laptop, a charger, and an AI tool you can log into. Set up your accounts before you arrive, because 75 minutes goes fast.

What will I build?

A fix for a real problem from your business. Building starts at 6:15 p.m. and demos start at 7:30 p.m.

Are there prizes?

Yes. After the demos, the best builds take 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place.

Where and when is it?

Thursday, December 10, 6 to 8 p.m. at Digital Harbor Tech Lab, 1045 Light St, Baltimore, MD 21230. Doors open at 6 p.m.

Who is behind the event?

The hackathon is sponsored by Knucklepuck, a DC-based digital marketing agency that sponsors B’more Tech so Baltimore’s founders and builders have a place to make things and meet each other.
